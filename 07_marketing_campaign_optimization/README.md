# Marketing Campaign Optimization & Conversion Prediction
**Level:** Advanced | **Task:** Classification + business analytics

## Objective
Predict campaign conversion probability and translate model outputs into targeting and campaign-optimization decisions.

## Workflow
1. Define conversion target and business cost/benefit assumptions.
2. Audit missing values, duplicates and leakage.
3. Build preprocessing pipeline for numeric/categorical features.
4. Establish logistic-regression baseline.
5. Compare tree-based model.
6. Evaluate ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, F1 and calibration.
7. Select a decision threshold using business cost assumptions.
8. Segment customers by predicted conversion probability.
9. Document limitations and fairness considerations.

## Portfolio value
Demonstrates the connection between ML modeling, marketing analytics, KPI optimization and business decision-making.
